trace-board

Search a username across the web. Confirmed accounts are found automatically where possible; the rest are one click away.
How to use this (30-second guide)
  1. Type a username (e.g. your own handle) and press Search.
  2. A green “found” tag means the account was confirmed automatically. A grey “open ↗” means the site can’t be checked from a browser — click the card to look yourself. A red “none” means confirmed not found.
  3. Want every site checked automatically? That needs the free Sherlock tool (one command). Run sherlock yourname, then click Import Sherlock file and choose the file it made — matching cards turn green.
Why can’t the browser check every site? Websites block other pages from reading them (a rule called CORS). trace-board auto-checks the sites that allow it and gives you a direct link for the rest — that’s the honest limit of any in-browser tool.
